## What is in a chapter

Each chapter explains the topic from the beginning, marks the definitions and
formulas that appear in the IB formula booklet, and works through numbered
examples in full. Practice follows every section as a **Your Turn** block, and
each chapter closes with end-of-chapter questions in Paper 1 and Paper 2 style
followed by Paper 3 style investigations. Higher Level content is marked.
