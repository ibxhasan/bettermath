## What is in a chapter

Each chapter builds the topic sequentially, focusing on intuition and deep
understanding. Definitions and formulas from the IB formula booklet are clearly
marked, followed by fully worked examples. Every section ends with a **Your
Turn** practice block for targeted review, and chapters conclude with Paper 1
and Paper 2 style questions — plus challenge problems for those who want to
push further. Higher Level content is marked.
