**Hasan Khan**

**Read them here: https://ibxhasan.github.io/bettermath/**

**[BetterMath — Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches HL](pdf/bettermath-aa-hl.pdf)**

and bookmarks throughout, so every chapter and section is one click away.

## What is in a chapter

Each chapter opens with a problem worth solving, then builds the topic in
order. Definitions and formulas from the IB formula booklet are marked.
Worked examples are numbered and complete. After every section there is a
**Your Turn** block of practice on that section alone, and the chapter ends
with end-of-chapter questions in Paper 1 and Paper 2 style, followed by
Paper 3 style investigations. Higher Level content is marked, so a Standard
Level reader can see what to skip.
