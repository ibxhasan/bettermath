What is in a chapter

Each chapter opens with a problem worth solving, then builds the topic in order. Definitions and formulas from the IB formula booklet are marked. Worked examples are numbered and complete. After every section there is a Your Turn block of practice on that section alone, and the chapter ends with end-of-chapter questions in Paper 1 and Paper 2 style, followed by Paper 3 style investigations.

Higher Level content is marked, so a Standard Level reader can see what to skip.

About

Hasan Khan has taught IB Mathematics AA HL for over ten years, is an IB examiner, and has worked as a national curriculum developer. He holds an M.Sc. in Particle Physics from the University of Jyväskylä, Finland, and a Postgraduate Diploma in Education. He has also completed teacher training in educational innovation with CERN, ESA and Wolfram.
