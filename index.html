The book

Intuitive. Clear. Uncompromising.

These notes skip shortcuts and artificial simplifications to focus on genuine clarity. Mastering AA HL takes dedication and practice; this book is here to support that effort, so you can give your whole attention to the mathematics.

Notes that explain why

Each chapter builds the topic in order, starting from the reason it exists. Formulas from the IB booklet are marked, and every idea comes with a fully worked example.

Your Turn, every section

A practice block closes each section, so you check an idea while it is fresh rather than at the end of the chapter.

Exam-style problems

Chapters end with Paper 1 and Paper 2 style questions, then challenge investigations for anyone who wants to go further.

About the author

Hasan Khan

HK

Hasan Khan has taught IB Mathematics AA HL for over ten years, is an IB examiner, and has worked as a national curriculum developer. He holds an M.Sc. in Particle Physics from the University of Jyväskylä, Finland, and a Postgraduate Diploma in Education, and has completed teacher training in educational innovation with CERN, ESA and Wolfram.
