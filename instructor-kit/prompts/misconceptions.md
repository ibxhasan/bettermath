# Prompt pack — Misconceptions & intervention

---

## A. Misconception bank for a topic

```
Course: [ ]
Topic + codes: [ ]
List the 8 most common student misconceptions.
For each:
- wrong idea (student wording)
- why it feels plausible
- a 2-minute classroom correction
- one probe question that reveals the misconception quickly
```
