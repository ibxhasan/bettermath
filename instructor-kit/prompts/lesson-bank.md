# Prompt pack — Lesson bank

Copy a block into Cursor Chat. Fill the bracketed fields.

## A. Standard 40-minute lesson

```
Act as an experienced IB Math teacher.
Course: [AA HL | AA SL | AI HL | AI SL]
Syllabus codes: [e.g. 2.5, 2.10]
Topic title: [e.g. Composite functions]
Lesson length: [40 | 60 | 80] minutes
Prior knowledge: [list]
Class profile: international school, mixed English levels; include 1 language scaffold

Output:
1) Fill instructor-kit/templates/lesson-plan.md
2) One projector-ready worked example (fully written)
3) A 15-minute practice set (mixed difficulty)
4) Exit ticket (2 questions, 5 minutes)
5) Teacher notes: misconceptions + what to circulate for

Flag anything that may be off-syllabus for this course/level.
```
